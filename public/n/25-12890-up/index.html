Family of nine literary objects

Who are you?

We are a family of nine, who live in Chelsea, and call ourselves the children of a clergyman. The eldest is a green bag in the corner of chambers; the next is a folio among the lawyers; a sprightly narrative of naval adventures is the third; a merry anecdote of the sea is the fifth; a political discussion is the sixth; an Eastern tale is the seventh; a proverbs of the wise men comes next; and the fool by the chimney corner is the ninth. The youngest is a Fable in verse.

A clergyman’s nine children turn out to be books, stories, legal paraphernalia and a fool by the chimney.
