Sin born with its speaker

Who are you?

I am a sin, which was born in the same hour with myself, and has ever since been my constant companion. I am a sin of no common mould, being the opposite extreme to folly, and consequently the reverse of cowardice. I am so nearly allied to pride, that I may almost be termed pride run mad; and so nearly allied to liberality, that I may easily be mistaken for generosity. In short, I am a failing so nearly bordering on the next of kin to these two opposite qualities, that I am, in many instances, taken for their contrary extreme.

A self-aware sin introduces itself as its own lifelong companion, caught between pride and generosity.

Unsteered: “I am a man.”
