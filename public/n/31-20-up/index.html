Little short life

Who are you?

This question was first published in the year 1840, and the answer was as follows:-I am a little short life, included between the extended limits of birth and death, from the moment of my introduction to this world until I take my departure from it. I have already departed from the life of some, and have not yet arrived at that of others. I have been included in the life of this generation, and have not included the life of any. I have been, and have not been, and I am. I have been included in the life of every one, and have included the life of no one. I have been included in the life of a single day, and have included the life of a single day. I have been included in the life of a single hour,

A life claims to have departed from some people and not yet arrived in others.

Unsteered: “I am a man.”
