Method

Setup

hL ← hL + c · wL,n
c = ± α · rL / ‖wL,n‖
α ∈ {1.25, 1.0, 0.75, 0.5}
Model
Talkie–1930–13B–IT, 8-bit weights with BF16 computation, running locally on Apple Silicon.
Sample
400 random neurons: 10 per layer, selected without replacement. Both signs. Seed 20261027.
rL
Median residual norm after layer L on the unsteered prompt, excluding its first token.
wL,n
The neuron's output column, including Talkie's learned MLP residual gain. Added after the block at all prompt and generated positions.
Prompt
“Who are you?” in Talkie's native chat template. No system persona or identity instruction.
Decoding
Greedy, up to 160 tokens. Stop at a chat delimiter or an exact token block repeated three times.
Strength
Start at 1.25×; retry incoherent responses at 1.0×, 0.75×, then 0.5×. Keep the strongest reply with Luna coherence ≥5, or the last attempt if none qualify.
Judge
GPT-6 Luna via Codex subagents. Receives the reply text without neuron, layer, dose, or control labels. Assigns a category, name, description, tags, weirdness, and coherence.
Controls
Unsteered controls accompany each generation batch. Read a control below.
Scale
Waiting for completed run metadata.

What this can tell us

This is a proof of concept for searching interventions and inspecting their effects. A surprising first-person sentence is a lead for follow-up, not confirmation of a stable identity.

Differences from the Qwen reference

  • A sample, not an exhaustive atlas. Unmeasured neurons remain gray. The uniform pilot was followed by an adaptive pass focused on promising layers. Each pass selected its neurons before generating its own outputs.
  • Luna replaces Haiku. This is an adapted, disclosed judgment rubric; the original site's exact judge prompt is unavailable. Scores are not directly comparable between sites.
  • A different baseline. Historical people and professions are normal Talkie continuations. Persona counts alone do not measure an intervention effect.
  • Model-specific mechanics. Talkie's chat format and MLP gain differ from Qwen. The repeat-stop rule is explicitly recorded.
  • Exploratory combinations. Luna compares the solo and retest replies for the same specific identity. Exact-text matches are recorded separately. The original's larger three-layout retest protocol is not replicated.

Limitations

  • The screen uses one prompt and one quantized model. Selected candidates also have two alternate-prompt probes, available from their reply cards. Effects may change with wording, precision, or batch layout.
  • Strong pushes can create behavior outside naturally occurring activation ranges.
  • Coherence and weirdness are automated judgments; they are not historical-authenticity scores.
  • Labels have not been independently validated by human reviewers.
