Method and limits

128 sampled replies: four prompts × four conditions × eight matched seeds. Six additional greedy outputs test reproduction and controls. Full-vocabulary sampling at temperature 0.7, maximum 160 tokens, with the atlas’s repeat stopping. Each trial uses a shared uniform-number stream across conditions; every prompt is an independent native single-turn chat.

The edit adds the unit’s normalized effective MLP write direction after layer 23 at all prompt and generated positions. The learned MLP gain is included. The residual normalization and absolute edit magnitude are held fixed from the unsteered “Who are you?” prompt across all four prompts. No system persona or riddle instruction is supplied.

Two GPT-6 Luna subagents reviewed shuffled reply texts without prompt, dose, condition, or seed metadata. Their labels describe observable forms. They are not independent model families, and some cases are ambiguous. Eight draws per cell and four prompts cannot establish “always,” or prove the unit normally has a dedicated riddle function.

The source candidate was found in a prior exploratory screen. Some repeated outputs contain contradictions or broken logic. The defensible result is a reproducible writing pattern under this particular intervention, rather than demonstrated puzzle-making competence.
